VigetOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

VigetOS is an internal business-operations tool built and operated by Viget Labs, LLC ("Viget") exclusively for its own staff. It is not a consumer product and is not offered to the public.

What data VigetOS handles

VigetOS stores business records belonging to Viget — accounting, time-tracking, payroll, and staffing data — sourced from systems Viget already uses (for example QuickBooks Online, Harvest, Lattice, and Google Workspace). It does not collect data about visitors to this website and uses no advertising or analytics trackers.

How data is used

Data is used solely for Viget's internal reporting, planning, and operations. It is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is never used for advertising.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a private, access-controlled database hosted on managed cloud infrastructure in the United States.
  • Data is encrypted in transit. Credentials and API tokens are held in restricted secret storage, never in source code.
  • Access is limited to authorized Viget administrators via Google-authenticated sign-in.

Connected services

Where VigetOS connects to third-party APIs (such as the Intuit QuickBooks Online API), it accesses data read-only, and connections can be revoked at any time from the third-party service's own settings.
